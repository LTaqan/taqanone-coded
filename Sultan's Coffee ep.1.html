Scene 1 — The Chrono-Crisis
Welcome to the Bureau of Temporal Corrections, Yusuf. You are our best agent.

We have a massive emergency. Yesterday, Bob—the world's most clumsy time-traveling tourist—was visiting Istanbul in the year 1555. This was the exact year Özdemir Pasha brought the first coffee beans from Yemen to the Ottoman Empire.

While Bob was drinking the historic first cup of coffee, he accidentally dropped his iPhone 15 Pro Max onto the merchant's table and ran back to the time portal!
Vocabulary Vault 1
If Bob leaves modern technology in the past, he will alter the course of human history. What does alter mean?
💡 Hint: If you give an iPhone to a 16th-century Sultan, the timeline will not stay the same.
📚 Vocabulary: Alter — To change something in a significant way.
Scene 2 — The Grand Bazaar
You step out of the portal into the bustling streets of 16th-century Istanbul.

You must find the coffee merchant before he shows the iPhone to Sultan Suleiman! You ask a local guard what happened yesterday. The guard replies: "It was crazy! A strange man was drinking a dark liquid when a loud buzzing noise suddenly scared him!"
Idiom Check
You say to the guard: "Please, don't hide the truth. It's time to spill the beans!" What are you asking him to do?
💡 Hint: "Spill the beans" is used when someone is keeping a secret.
📚 Vocabulary: Bustling — Full of energetic and noisy activity.
☕ Idiom: Spill the beans — To reveal secret information.
Scene 3 — Grammar Timeout
The guard used two different tenses to describe the chaotic event!
Past Continuous vs. Past Simple
Past Continuous (was/were + V-ing) = long background action.
Past Simple (V2) = short, sudden action that interrupts it.
Grammar Challenge
Choose the correct verb forms to fill in the blanks:

"The merchants _________________ (argue) over the price of silk when Bob's iPhone alarm suddenly _________________ (ring) loudly."
💡 Hint: Arguing takes time (continuous). Ringing is sudden (simple).
Scene 4 — The Magic Mirror
You find the coffeehouse. Hakem the merchant is holding the iPhone, watching a video of a cat playing a piano.

"Behold!" Hakem shouts. "This tiny, glowing painting is alive! I shall give it to the Sultan!"

You ask for it back, but he refuses. "This is a priceless artifact," he says.
True or False
Statement 1: If Hakem says the phone is "priceless," it means it is completely worthless.
Statement 2: Hakem thinks the phone is magic. You should explain that it is Apple technology to get it back.
💎 Vocabulary: Priceless — So precious its value cannot be determined!
Scene 5 — The Trade Agreement
Hakem looks at you. "I will only trade this magic mirror if you give me something from the future."

You have three items: A plastic credit card, a digital watch, and a paper map of global trade routes.

Giving the wrong item could cause a storm in a teacup or destroy history!
Logic Challenge
What is the safest, most valuable trade for an Ottoman merchant that won't break the timeline?
🌪️ Idiom: A storm in a teacup — Unnecessary worry about a matter that is not important.
Scene 6 — Beating the Clock
Hakem looks at the map. His eyes widen. "Incredible!"

He hands over the iPhone just as the battery dies. You hear the Sultan's guards approaching. You have to beat the clock! You jump back into the time portal, saving the 16th century!
